ABOUT THE PROJECT

A thoughtful approach to growing.

AgriCycle is a student project that explains the role of crop rotation in building more sustainable farming systems.

Crop rotation is the practice of growing different crops in sequence on the same field over different seasons or years. Instead of repeatedly planting the same crop, a farmer plans a suitable sequence based on crop families, soil conditions, climate and available water.

WHY CROP ROTATION?

Benefits that go beyond harvest.

When designed for local conditions, crop rotation can support soil function, crop diversity and integrated farm management.

01
◈

Soil health

Different root systems and crop residues can contribute to soil structure and organic matter management.

02
＋

Nutrient cycling

Legumes can add biologically fixed nitrogen to the farming system when properly managed.

03
⌁

Pest management

Changing crop families can disrupt some pest and disease cycles that depend on particular host crops.

04
❋

Crop diversity

Growing a wider range of crops can support diversity within the farm ecosystem.

05
◌

Resource planning

Seasonal crop choices can be matched with available water, labour and other farm resources.

Long-term resilience

Well-planned rotations can contribute to a more adaptable and sustainable farming system.
